# Code of conduct

Noise Filter is built by and for people who just want to be heard clearly. We want taking part in
this project to feel the same way: respectful, welcoming and focused on the work.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers and with people writing in a second language.
  English and Bahasa Indonesia are both welcome in issues.
- Discuss ideas, not people. Assume good intent and ask before concluding.
- Accept that maintainers make the final call on what goes into the project, and explain your
  reasoning when you disagree.
- Respect privacy. Don't post other people's recordings, personal details or private messages.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, discriminatory jokes or language, sexual content, threats, doxxing, and
sustained disruption of discussions, in any project space (issues, pull requests, discussions,
and other channels where you represent the project).

## Reporting

Report a problem to the maintainers through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/SkillsHUB-Electronics/Noise-Filter/security/advisories/new)
(choose it even though it is not a security issue) or by contacting the repository owner on GitHub.
Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close issues, or block people who break this code, and
will explain the decision where it is safe to do so.

This code is adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
