VB-CABLE Virtual Audio Device
=============================
This installer includes VB-CABLE, a virtual audio cable made by VB-Audio Software
(https://www.vb-cable.com). Noise Filter sends the cleaned microphone sound to
"CABLE Input"; other apps (Zoom, Meet, TeamTalk) then use "CABLE Output" as their microphone.

VB-CABLE is a donationware, all participations are welcome.
Donate or buy a license: https://shop.vb-audio.com
